Frequently asked questions

Is the jewelry waterproof?

Yes! Our jewelry is crafted from super-strong 316L Stainless Steel. This material is ultra-durable, resisting tarnishing and fading better than silver, gold, or platinum. It stays shiny and won't cause skin irritation.

Wondering what makes it waterproof? We use an advanced process called PVD (Physical Vapor Deposition), which is ten times stronger than standard coatings.

We're not just saying our jewelry is water-resistant for show – we truly mean it! Our pieces have been tried and loved by countless happy customers.

Still need convincing? Take a look at our Trustpilot reviews!

Can I track my order?

Yes, of course!

From the start that your order is processed you will receive a confirmation email followed by a notification email to inform you that your item has been dispatched.

In this email, you will find your tracking number.

What is your refund policy?

Yes — we offer full refunds. You have 60 days from delivery to return your order, and EU and UK customers also have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal, no reason needed.

You may inspect and try on your item as you would in a shop; we only deduct for any reduction in value caused by handling beyond that. Refunds are issued within 14 days of us receiving your return.

For more information read our Refund Policy.

Can I change or cancel my order?

You can cancel your order any time before it ships. Because we pack orders quickly, please contact us as soon as possible at info@sava-amsterdam.com, or use the returns and cancellations option in your account.

If your order has already shipped, you can still return it for a refund within 60 days. EU and UK customers have a statutory 14-day right of withdrawal from delivery.

Please follow the steps in our Refund Policy.

How long does shipping take?

Orders placed before 22:45 on business days will be dispatched the next day. Shipping times typically range from 5-7 business days, depending on the destination.

Do you ship worldwide?

Yes, we ship internationally for free!
